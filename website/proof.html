ADR-01

Bi-Temporal Supersession over Append-Only RAG

Every TemporalFact tracks valid_from, valid_until, and superseded_by. Upserting a singular=True predicate atomically retires prior values while retaining historical time travel.

ADR-02

Hybrid BM25 + 2-Hop Activation over Embeddings

Code-aware tokenization splits CamelCase and snake_case identifiers, boosts exact matches, activates 2-hop neighbors, and packs deterministically in sub-6ms without cloud embedding calls.
