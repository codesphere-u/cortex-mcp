Frequently Asked Questions

Engineering design details

Why bi-temporal tracking instead of a flat vector store?

Vector similarity treats deprecated config (like JWT_HS256) and its replacement (PASETO_v4_public) as equally true neighbors. Bi-temporal bounds (valid_from, valid_until, superseded_by) exclude superseded facts while preserving historical time travel via memory_time_travel.

How does Cortex-MCP integrate with TruthGate and hosted MCP clients?

cortex-mcp export-sources emits verified GroundedSourceDocument payloads for citation verification, while api/index.py exposes Streamable HTTP at POST /mcp and local agents connect over stdio or HTTP/SSE.

How is tampering detected in local graph storage?

Every recorded episode and fact participates in a SHA-256 hash chain and deterministic Merkle root digest checked by memory_verify_integrity.
